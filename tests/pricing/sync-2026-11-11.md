## Screen source decision and unmapped entries

- The shop selected **Screen Repair Pricing** as the screen-price authority. All listed screen rows were compared with the website; four additional OLED prices changed: Air $379 → $400, 17 $229 → $250, 17 Pro $249 → $250, and 17 Pro Max $299 → $300. Other listed OLED/iPad screen prices already matched. Older models absent from this sheet retain their existing prices.
- The shop explicitly instructed us to ignore all `Refurb/OEM` listings. None is exposed as a repair option. Factory-OLED iPhones use only the OLED column, even where the sheet also contains a cheaper Screen-column price.
- Blanks do not mean zero, deletion or an authorization to remove a repair. Existing iPhone battery, port, camera-glass and back-housing prices already match the populated main-sheet cells.
- iPad 10 prices match the website's existing combined 10/11 entry; no model identifiers were changed. LCD-only tablet service prices absent from the spreadsheet remain unchanged.
- COMMON REPAIRS contains labor/parts formulas, not complete device-specific retail totals. Existing matching base amounts were retained. The soldered-port labor ($200), drive replacement with migration ($180), and other labor-only rows do not have exact existing standalone service mappings; they were not substituted for all-in repair estimates. Those require a separate service/parts presentation decision if the shop wants them added.
- Trade-in columns are not repair prices and were not published.
- Diagnostics, consoles, controllers, computers and other unrelated prices remain unchanged.
